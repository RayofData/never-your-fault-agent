# Latest League of Legends Patch

**Patch:** 26.15

**Published:** July 28, 2026

The recent updates have significantly enhanced the abilities, cooldowns, health restoration rates, damage ratios, and other features across various characters in League of Legends:

1. **Base Health Restored**: For Purgatory's R - Paranoia Cooldown Refund:
   - Normal Range (40 / 70 / 100 / 130 / 160): Updated to a refund range from 50% to 75%.
   
2. **Passive Abilities & Damage Ratios**
   - Samira’s Taunt Gold Exchange: This feature has been removed entirely.
   - Tahm Kench's E ability, Thick Skin:
     - Normal Range (15 / 47 %): Updated damage percentage range from minimum of 23% to maximum still at 47%.
     
3. **Cooldowns & Durations** 
   - Thresh’s W Passive – Damnation Souls Gained on Pickup: Soul gain increase adjusted by a multiplier factor.
   - Twisted Fate's R and E abilities, Destiny Cooldown:
     - Normal Range (170 / 140 / 110): Adjusted cooldown to normal range from minimum of 40 seconds up to maximum at the lowest value.

4. **Damage Modifiers**
   - Lich Bane: Attack Speed increase adjusted by a percentage.
   - Samira’s Q ability – Primal Howl Damage Reduction (Normal Range increased).

5. **Cooldown Adjustments and Health Restoration** 
   - Tahm Kench's R cooldown adjustments for E, Flay:
     - Normal range from 120 / 100 / 80 seconds: Updated to normal duration with minimum at least reduced by half.
     
6. **New Leveling Features**
   - Zac’s W Passive – Unstable Matter Arena Cooldown has been decreased significantly.

7. **Character-Specific Fixes & Quality of Life Improvements** 
   - Neeko's disguise abilities have had several bugs fixed, allowing more flexibility in disguises and interactions with other characters.
   
8. **Upcoming Skins**
   - New skins for Darius (Divine God-King) are being released alongside the return to The Sanctum until Patch 26.19.

These updates aim at enhancing gameplay experience by making abilities faster or stronger, reducing cooldown times significantly in some cases which can increase team’s pressure and dynamism during matches while also fixing numerous bugs that affected character interactions with other players' skins as well their unique mechanics.
